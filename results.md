# Results & Conclusion

## Verified test result in the supplied project

The supplied notebook reports a **25 August 2026 NO₂ test** over the Karachi study area:

- finite pixels: **419**
- total raster cells: **468**
- non-finite cells: **49**
- mean NO₂ column density: **3.3027845 × 10⁻⁵ mol/m²**
- minimum: **1.26051345 × 10⁻⁵ mol/m²**
- maximum: **8.6132815 × 10⁻⁵ mol/m²**
- Q1: **2.1488436687 × 10⁻⁵ mol/m²**
- median: approximately **2.8 × 10⁻⁵ mol/m²**
- Q3: **4.3069043386 × 10⁻⁵ mol/m²**
- IQR: **2.1580606699 × 10⁻⁵ mol/m²**
- IQR upper bound: **7.5439953434 × 10⁻⁵ mol/m²**
- possible IQR outliers: **3 pixels**

These values are transcribed from the supplied project outputs and summary CSV; they are **not independently recomputable from the archive** because the underlying 419-pixel raster/CSV is missing.
